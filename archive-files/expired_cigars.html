Expired Cigars - Updated November 2026

Artist's Statement

Broke, bored, and stuck between classes, we did what any responsible college students would do. We talked our friend Arman into taking expired cigars from his uncle’s shop and set up an afternoon to gamble.

Now, this was a problem, since three of the five of us are Muslim. But as Arman put it, “One man should not judge another. That is his conversation with Allah.” Plus, I wasn’t about to argue with the serial assaulter. And before you ask, yes, that’s a fair title. The man has personally fought off multiple armed robberies at his 7-Eleven and his uncle’s shop. You don’t say no to that guy. You say “thank you for the cigars.”

Then, like every rotation, there was Abed, who hogged the cigars and came down with a serious case of cigar sickness. Pale, sweaty, barely holding it together, and he still asked if there were any more.

Zachery is a football player, which explains a lot. Every Halloween he puts on a ski mask and rams through people’s fences for fun. He brought that same energy to the table, going all in on every hand and losing every one.

And then there was Arham Amir. The whole afternoon, Arham refused to bet a single dollar. “I’m Muslim, bro. I can’t gamble.” Very admirable. He then bet a dare, someone’s AirPods, and Zachery’s watch.

He won the watch.

Good times. Stupid adults we were.

— Fried Chicken Connoisseur
