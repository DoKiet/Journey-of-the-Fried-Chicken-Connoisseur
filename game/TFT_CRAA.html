Why Poorer Players Shouldn’t Play the Same Board (Very Sorry for the Limited Model)

Weakness - The model is useful for showing how the differences in gold affect the execution of building the same board. However, it relies on strong simplifying assumptions, which limit its direct applicability to TFT gameplay.

  1. Does not account for RNG/Timing (so basically a poor player can high roll or just get better items and beat the rich player)
  2. Assumes that players behave all identically (Beta, r, u(s) = log(s)) (which is not true since some players roll early and snowball while others like to save and spend later)
  3. Does not account for Game Theory (no player reaction towards each other like pivots, tempo, denying units, contesting strategically)
  4. Treats boards like fixed cost strategies rather than viewing them as flexible
  5. Assumes (R) will always have better board (even if Sr > Sp, reason (1) can happen — just because R spends more doesn't mean P can't play the same board)
  6. Assumes smooth growth and gradual spending (players are not maximizing intertemporal utility — they are playing for placement spikes)
