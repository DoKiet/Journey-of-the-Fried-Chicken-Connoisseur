Comprehensive Guide to Understanding the Original Tsukihime

About This Guide

This is a personal guide made to help readers better understand the original Tsukihime, its lore, themes, and route interpretations. It includes discussion of the main routes, character analysis, and commentary on related material such as Kagetsu Tohya and Melty Blood.

The guide is written with heavy spoilers and is best read after finishing the story.

What’s Inside

  1. Context before the story, including Roa, True Ancestors, and the Counter Force
  2. Explanations of major background lore and family history
  3. Route-by-route interpretations for Arcueid, Ciel, Hisui, Akiha, and Kohaku
  4. Thematic discussion of guilt, identity, repression, acceptance, and tragedy
  5. Personal commentary on execution, characters, and storytelling strengths and flaws

Note

This guide is written in a personal and informal style, mixing analysis, criticism, humor, and recommendations for readers who want a deeper dive into the Tsukihime series.
